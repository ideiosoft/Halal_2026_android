## 5. Scan-usage / subscription / history APIs

**Base URL:** `https://admin.halaladvisor.com.au/`
**Definition:** `productscan/ScanUsageApi.kt` · **Client:** `ScanUsageApiClient` (Retrofit + RxJava2, 15s timeouts)

| # | Purpose | Method | Endpoint | Request | Response (key fields) |
|---|---------|--------|----------|---------|------------------------|
| 1 | Get scan usage | `GET`  | `api/scan_usage/{userId}` | path: `userId` | `usedScans, maxScans, remainingScans, resetDate` |
| 2 | Increment scan count | `POST` | `api/increment_scan` | body: `{ u_id }` | `success, message, data{ usedScans, maxScans, remainingScans, resetDate }` |
| 3 | Add to history | `POST` | `api/history_add` | body: `{ u_id, barcode, productName, brand, halalStatus }` | `success, message` |
| 4 | Get history list | `GET`  | `api/history_list/{userId}` | path: `userId`; query: `page` (def 1), `limit` (def 20) | `items[]{ id, barcode, product_name, brand, halal_status, scanned_at }`, `pagination{ page, limit, totalItems, totalPages }` |
| 5 | Get total counts (summary) | `GET`  | `api/history_summary/{userId}` | path: `userId` | `totalScans, halalCount, haramCount, mushboohCount` |
| 6 | Get subscription status | `GET`  | `api/subscription_status/{userId}` | path: `userId` | `isPremium, planName, expiryDate, autoRenew` |

**Flow:** GET scan usage (gate scan limit) → on scan, POST increment_scan (count++) + POST history_add → history_list (recent scans / View all) + history_summary (total counts) → subscription_status (premium = unlimited, no ads).
